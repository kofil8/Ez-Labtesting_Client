
## **ACTUAL UX ISSUES ON YOUR HOMEPAGE**

### **1. HERO IS VISUALLY WEAK - No Emotional Connection**

**What's there:**
- Generic headline "Online Lab Testing, Simplified"
- Lab tech image (professional but cold)
- Search box as primary CTA
- Discount banner above (10% off)

**The Real Problem:**
- Visitor lands and doesn't feel **reassured** or **motivated** to act
- Image of lab tech is clinical, not patient-relatable
- Search box placement says "find something" but new user has no idea WHAT to search for
- Generic messaging could be ANY lab testing site

**What It Should Do:**
```
CURRENT: Cold, corporate, transactional
SHOULD BE: Warm, relatable, benefit-driven

Headline should answer: "Why should I care?"
  NOT: "Online Lab Testing, Simplified"
  YES: "Know Your Health Without Leaving Home"
       "Get Answers Your Doctor Trusts"
       "Lab Tests in 24 Hours, Results in 48"

Hero image: Should show a PATIENT benefit
  NOT: Lab tech at work bench (clinical anxiety)
  YES: Person checking results on phone (empowerment)
       OR: Young person feeling energized (outcome)
```

---

### **2. SEARCH EXPERIENCE - Assumes User Knowledge**

**Issue:**
- Text search with "Popular searches: CBC, Thyroid Panel, STD Screen"
- User who feels "exhausted" doesn't know if that's a "CBC" or something else
- No symptom-to-test guidance

**Real Problem:**
```
USER JOURNEY:
"I feel tired" 
  ↓
[Search for...?] ← User is lost
  ↓
Types "tired" → Returns nothing
  ↓
Leaves site

SHOULD BE:
"I feel tired"
  ↓
[Symptom Selector] ← Click what applies
  ├─ Fatigue/Exhaustion
  ├─ Brain Fog
  ├─ Low Energy
  ↓
[Recommended Tests Appear]
  ├─ Complete Blood Count ($45)
  ├─ Thyroid Panel ($64)
  ├─ Vitamin D Test ($64)
  ↓
[Add to Cart] OR [Learn Why These]
```
